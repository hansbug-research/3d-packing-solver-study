Source findings
---------------
crates/d3/src/packer.rs layer_packing and
crates/d3/src/packing_utils.rs layer_place_items reset X/Y and advance Z after
a Y overflow, then check only Z before accepting the placement. They do not
recheck the oriented width and depth after the reset.

crates/d3/src/packer.rs builds GA/BRKGA/SA runner configs without forwarding
Config.seed or Config.time_limit_ms. Their run paths use rand::rng().
ExtremePoint also receives no deadline from Config.time_limit_ms.
